ApplyFill settings

Your details and saved answers. They stay in this browser and are only sent to Claude when you ask it to draft an answer or set up from your CV.

Start here

Paste your CV as plain text. With an Anthropic API key, ApplyFill can fill in your details and write your saved answers from it, and open questions get a "Draft with Claude" button. Without a key, fill the sections below by hand. Your key is stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic. Get one at console.anthropic.com.

Your details

Used for short fields like name, email and notice period. Leave a field blank to skip it. For right to work and sponsorship, write Yes or No so radio buttons and dropdowns can be picked for you.

Saved answers

For open questions. "Matches" is a comma-separated list of words; a question containing any of them gets this answer suggested. Use {company} and {role} and they'll be filled in from the page. Replace any [bracketed] text with your own.

Back up

Export everything to a JSON file (your API key is left out), or restore from one.

Fill log

Each time you fill a saved answer or a Claude draft, ApplyFill notes the company, role and which answer you used. Export it and press Import ApplyFill log in your AI Role Tracker to compare reply rates per answer. It stays in this browser until you export it.
